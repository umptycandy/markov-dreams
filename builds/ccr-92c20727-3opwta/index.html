Markov Dreams

A small band on the FM-1 that will learn what you play. Work in progress: step 1 of 5.

The FM-1

Turn your phone sideways for bigger controls.

Tab moves through the knobs, the buttons, the black keys and the white keys. Arrow keys turn a knob, Page Up and Page Down faster. Space or Enter holds a button or key down while you hold it; Shift and Enter keeps it down until you press it again (with a mouse: Shift-click or right-click).

Off. Sound starts when you switch it on. Forget what was saved and start again from the factory settings

How to play (step 1)

  • Switch on, then PLAY.
  • FX / SEL / ENV / LFO pick a track: DRM, BAS, LED, CHD. Tap DRM again for kick, snare, hat.
  • The keys play the chosen track.
  • Drag PRESETS up or down to change the kit. SELECT sets the tempo.
  • Hold any button for a short help card.

On a phone

Turn it sideways. Drag a knob up or down to turn it. If there is no sound, check the phone is not on silent.
