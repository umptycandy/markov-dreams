MARKOV DREAMS

A small generative band for the M-VAVE FM-1.

It learns what you play, then keeps playing in your style.

TAP TO POWER ON
Based on Mutable Dreams · GPL-3.0 · the real FM-1 firmware, running in your browser

HOW TO PLAY

Listen: press PLAY. Turn BRAIN to change style and KIT to change the band. To turn a knob, drag up or down on it (or scroll over it).

Teach: pick a track (DRM, BAS, LED or CHD), press TEACH and play along on the keys while it loops. Press TEACH again, and it improvises in your style. On drums, low keys are kick, middle keys snare and high keys hats. Choose the BLANK brain to teach from scratch.

WILDLeft = sticks to what it learned. Right = surprises and stutters.
DENSITYFewer or more notes.
TONEDarker or brighter, for the whole band.
CLOUDGrain cloud and room on lead and chords (drums and bass stay dry).
HOLD A TRACKHold DRM, BAS, LED or CHD and turn knobs to shape that track's sound. KNOB 1 picks the engine (on drums: the drum sound), KNOBS 2–4 are TONE, HARM and DECAY. A long press keeps the sound page open; tap the button again to close it. Tap DRM again to step kick → snare → hat.
KITLoads a whole band: a sound for every track. Your knobs, tempo, root and scale stay. A * after the kit name means you've shaped a sound.
ARPChip arpeggios for the selected track (not drums).
GLOGlobal page: the four knobs set SWING, ROOT, SCALE and PUMP (the kick ducks the rest). Press GLO again to go back.
FREEZELock the current bar into a loop. Press again to let it evolve.
SAVEStop first. Keeps the kit and your knobs in this browser. Taught brains are not saved yet.
Hold any other buttonA short help card on the screen.

The lights on the white keys show the selected track's 16 steps. On a computer, the Z and Q letter rows play the white keys and the rows above them play the black keys. Space = play, Enter = teach.
