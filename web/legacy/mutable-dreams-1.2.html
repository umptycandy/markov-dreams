Begin with Blank or any factory Biome. Shape your six voices and phrases, then use GLO → User Banks to select one of 16 Biome slots or 64 sound slots. Enter a name, stop playback, choose internal clock and save. In browser mode, an occupied slot requires a second Save within three seconds. The live FM-1 editor instead asks you to confirm replacement. Browser banks stay in this browser; hardware banks stay on the FM-1. Export each finished Biome as a file for safekeeping. GROWING THROUGH YOUR FEEDBACK

Thank you for listening.

Your response to Mutable Dreams has shaped this next chapter. Your requests for warmer sounds, personal Biomes and dependable performance are at the heart of version 1.2. Thank you for making music with it, and for telling us what could be better.

Every voice is yours.

Fill all six tracks with percussion, resonant strings, soft chords, or any combination. Choose from 288 sound presets while keeping your notes, rhythm, levels and sends. An empty Biome is a starting point for a world of your own.

Chance, with a memory.

Euclidean patterns, swing, accents and repeats meet eight shared scales. Lock a seed with ARP, make a variation with REC, and undo it with ENV. Freeze, reverse or transpose the granular cloud as your rhythm keeps moving.

Room to be clear.

Full-rate granular recording, gently overlapping grains and a cleaner signal path bring more detail. Open the filter for clarity or close it for warmth. Gentle resonance and fundamental-led presets keep softer sounds within easy reach. FULL and ECO offer different processing budgets. Automatic admission limits remain in place. The release passed 29 controlled FM-1 cases; dense patches may require ECO. See the qualification report for scope and limits.

WITH GRATITUDE / OUR ROOTS

From a generous idea,
new worlds can grow.

Mutable Dreams owes its foundations to Émilie Gillet and Mutable Instruments. Their instruments and openly shared code made this exploration possible. Selected original algorithms from Plaits, Rings and the new Elements Strike subset form part of our sound engine, with their original credits and MIT notices preserved.

Our contribution is a six-voice instrument for the FM-1: editable Biomes, generative rhythms, a custom granular bus, and an interface that invites discovery. We want to carry that spirit of curiosity into something approachable, expressive, and unmistakably its own.
