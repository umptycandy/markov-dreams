MARKOV SID

Generative chip groovebox · FM-1 firmware prototype

It learns what you play, then keeps playing in your style.

TAP TO POWER ON

HOW TO PLAY

Listen: press PLAY. Turn BRAIN to change style and KIT to change sound. To turn a knob, drag up or down on it (or scroll over it).

Teach: pick a track (DRM, BAS, LED or CHD), press TEACH and play along on the keys while it loops. Press TEACH again, and it improvises in your style. On drums, low keys are kick, middle keys snare and high keys hats. Choose the BLANK brain to teach from scratch.

WILDLeft = sticks to what it learned. Right = surprises and stutters.
DENSITYFewer or more notes.
DIRTClean C64 to blown-out: drive, bit-crush, lo-fi.
VERB+DLYReverb and ping-pong delay on lead and chords (drums and bass stay dry).
HOLD A TRACKHold DRM, BAS, LED or CHD and turn knobs to shape that track's sound. KNOB 1 picks the engine (CHIP, FM, and ANALOG for drums), and KNOBS 2–4 are its tone controls. With a mouse, long-press the track button to keep the sound page open, then tap it again to close.
KITLoads a full band: an engine and sound for every track. A * after the kit name means you've tweaked it.
ARPToggle chip arps for the selected track. Chords with ARP off play as power chords.
GLOGlobal page: the four knobs set SWING, ROOT, SCALE and PUMP (sidechain ducking). Press GLO again to go back.
FREEZELock the current bar into a loop. Press again to let it evolve.
Greyed buttonsNot used yet: room to expand.

The lights on the white keys show the selected track's 16 steps. Your brains are kept in this browser. On a computer, the Z and Q letter rows play the white keys and the rows above them play the black keys. Space = play, Enter = teach.
